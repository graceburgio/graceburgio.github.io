Privacy Policy: Golden Gate Ship Alerts

Golden Gate Ship Alerts is used only by the owner of the @GoldenGateLive YouTube channel. It uses Google sign-in solely to post ship-passing messages to that channel's live chat.

It does not collect, store, sell, or share any data about viewers or other users. Ship position data comes from the public AIS feed at aisstream.io.

Questions: contact the channel at youtube.com/@GoldenGateLive.
